---
title: Introducing LuaDocs
description: Why standard Lua deserves a reference you can read, search, and run.
date: 2026-08-06
---

The official Lua manual is a single enormous page. It is complete, precise, and
written by the people who built the language — and it is very hard to read if you are
looking for one function while holding a half-finished idea in your head.

LuaDocs is a rewrite of that manual as a reference site: one entry per function,
operator and concept, written in our own words, with a link back to the passage of the
manual it came from. Every entry says which versions of Lua it applies to, from 5.1
through 5.5, and most examples run in the browser.

None of this replaces the manual. It is the same material, arranged for the way people
actually read documentation.
