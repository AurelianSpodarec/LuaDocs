---
title: The day lua.org went offline
description: What an outage upstream means for a site rewritten from the manual.
date: 2026-08-07
---

While writing entries today, `lua.org` stopped responding. Not slow — no response at
all, on either port, while every other host we tried answered normally.

That is worth sitting with, because every entry on this site carries a source link back
to the passage of the manual it was written from. When the manual is unreachable, those
links go nowhere, and the process of writing new entries stops too.

The manual ships inside the Lua source distribution, and it carries its own licence
notice placing it under the same terms as Lua itself. So a copy can be kept alongside
the site: not as a replacement for the original, which stays the thing we cite, but so
that neither the writing nor the reading depends on one host being up.
